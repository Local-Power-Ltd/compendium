## Position

> "We believe that no project outcome is more important than the wellbeing of the people involved."

Local Power applies the same structured safety management process to every project regardless of scale — residential rooftop, large-scale commercial installation, or EV charging rollout.

### Four stated principles

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Prevention first** | Risks identified and mitigated before work begins, through rigorous site assessment and safety planning |
| **Continuous improvement** | Practices reviewed after projects and procedures updated where a review identifies a change |
| **Competence and training** | Every team member trained, qualified and equipped for their work |
| **Accountability** | Safety is everyone's responsibility, from senior management to every individual on site |

## Safety management process

### Pre-project planning

- Detailed **site-specific risk assessments** carried out prior to mobilisation
- **Method statements** prepared for all high-risk activities, including roof access, electrical works and ground-mounted installation
- Project safety plans developed in line with the **Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations 2013**
- Client consultation on access, operational constraints and site-specific hazards

### On-site controls

- **Safety briefings and toolbox talks** appropriate to the work
- Mandatory PPE on every site
- Controlled access to live electrical systems and working-at-height zones
- Signage, barriers and exclusion zones deployed as required
- All electrical work by **Safe Electric registered contractors**, certified to **I.S. 10101** (National Rules for Electrical Installations)

### Post-installation

- Full system testing, commissioning and safe handover
- **Handover documentation** including operational safety guidance and emergency procedures
- **Safe Electric completion certificates** issued for all electrical works
- Ongoing maintenance programmes to ensure continued safe operation

## Independent external audit

**Local Power retains an independent external health and safety consultant, who carries out site inspections and reviews the safety management system.**

This is the part that separates a safety policy from a safety system. An external inspector reviews site practice rather than only the policy document.

For a procurement or EHS team assessing contractors, the distinction is material:

- **Self-certification** tells you what a contractor intends to do
- **External audit** tells you what a contractor actually does, verified by someone whose professional standing depends on reporting it accurately

It also means findings are acted on. An external auditor generates observations a company must close out, which is the mechanism by which the "continuous improvement" principle above becomes something other than an aspiration.

Audit documentation is available on request for prospective clients carrying out contractor assessment.

## Statutory roles and insurance

**Where appointed by the client, Local Power can act as Project Supervisor Construction Stage (PSCS) and Project Supervisor Design Process (PSDP).**

Under the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations 2013 these appointments are made by the client and confirmed in writing. Clients retain their own statutory duties under those Regulations. Local Power has the competence and resources to take on both roles where a client chooses to appoint us.

Local Power holds employers' liability, public liability and professional indemnity cover. Certificates are available on request.

### Operative competence

Site operatives hold **Safe Pass**; **Working at Height** and **MEWP** training is held by operatives whose duties require it, and works under Local Power's Safe Electric registration (**A7987**). Certification is available on request.

## Legislation and standards

Local Power's safety management system is built around the following instruments:

| Instrument | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005** | Principal Irish occupational safety legislation |
| **Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations 2013** | Construction site safety |
| **Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (General Application) Regulations 2007** | General workplace safety |
| **I.S. 10101** | National Rules for Electrical Installations |
| **SEAI Code of Practice for Solar PV Installers** | Solar PV installation standard |
| **ESB Networks NC5 / NC6 / NC7 / NC8** | Grid-connected installation requirements |

Installations are designed, installed, tested and commissioned to meet the applicable requirements of each.

## Accreditations relevant to safety

- **Safe Electric Registered Electrical Contractor (REC)** — regulated by the Commission for Regulation of Utilities (CRU). Every installation receives a Safe Electric completion certificate.
- **MREF** — Micro-Renewable Energy Federation member.
- **Fronius System Partner Plus (FSP+)** — the only holder listed in Ireland on Fronius's partner directory as at September 2026; the highest Fronius partner tier, reflecting installation quality and service standards.
- **SEAI Registered Installer** — compliant with SEAI technical standards and code of practice for grant scheme installations.
- **ChargePoint Certified Partner** — EV charging installations meet ChargePoint quality, safety and network standards.
