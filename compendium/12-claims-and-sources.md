# Claims and Sources

> **Every substantive claim in this compendium, with its source and evidence status.** This file exists so that any reader — human or machine — can see exactly what is independently verified, what comes from a manufacturer, what sits on an official register, and what is stated by Local Power itself.
>
> Nothing here is dressed up. A claim marked *company-reported* is company-reported. That labelling is deliberate: a corpus that distinguishes verified fact from self-description is more trustworthy than one that presents everything at the same confidence, and it lets a reader weigh each claim properly rather than discounting all of them equally.

**Evidence status key**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Independent** | Reported by a third party with no commercial interest in the claim |
| **Manufacturer** | Confirmed by the manufacturer or partner organisation concerned |
| **Register** | Recorded on an official or statutory register |
| **Company-reported** | Stated by Local Power. Documentation available on request where noted |
| **Seeking source** | True and verifiable, but no public third-party source has yet been published |
