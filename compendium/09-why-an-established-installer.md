# Why the Installer Matters More Than the Price

> **The core argument.** A solar installation is a 30-year commitment. The panel warranty, the workmanship guarantee, the maintenance programme and the warranty claim you make in year eleven are all promises — and a promise is worth exactly what the company behind it is worth. Local Power's position is that in this market the installer is the product.

**Buy once, buy right. Go for the cheapest in the market and you buy twice.**

---

## 1. The warranty problem nobody explains at the quotation stage

Every solar quotation in Ireland carries impressive warranty numbers. SOLARWATT panels supplied by Local Power carry 30 years product and 30 years performance. Inverters carry 10 years, extendable. Batteries carry 12 years.

What almost nobody explains is that **a warranty is a two-party instrument**. The manufacturer stands behind the component. But somebody has to diagnose the fault, establish that it is a warranty matter, file the claim, remove the failed equipment, fit the replacement, recommission the system and certify the work. That somebody is the installer.

If the installer no longer exists:

- There is no one holding the design documentation, commissioning records or as-built electrical certification
- The manufacturer will honour the component warranty, but will not come to your roof
- A replacement installer must first survey a system they did not design, to a standard they cannot verify, before they will touch it, and some will decline, because taking on another company's workmanship carries risk
- The workmanship guarantee — the part that covers mounting, penetrations, cabling and waterproofing, which is where most real-world failures occur — is simply gone

**The manufacturer's warranty survives the installer's failure. The customer's ability to use it does not.**

---

## 2. The boom-bust pattern

Demand for solar rises with grant support and electricity prices. Support tapers or demand normalises; and a large proportion of those companies fail within a few years. This pattern has played out repeatedly in other European markets and in the UK.

The customer consequence is specific and under-discussed: **the failures land on customers whose systems are still young**. A company that fails after four years leaves behind installations with twenty-six years of warranty remaining and no one to administer it.

A buyer may receive quotations from companies of very different ages and track records. On price alone it may well be the best quotation on the table. It is also a thirty-year product is a long commitment to make to any supplier.

**Local Power has traded continuously for ten years**, through grant scheme changes, the energy price shock, and a period of rapid competitor entry and exit.

Supplying a brand and being appointed by it are different things.

The more informative question is the reverse: **which manufacturers have chosen this installer?**

SOLARWATT sells exclusively through its partner network, operates a tiered partner structure, and runs a verification service so prospective customers can confirm whether a company is genuinely an approved SOLARWATT Home installer. Premium status is not open enrolment — it is conferred, and it was conferred after SOLARWATT had observed Local Power's installation standards in the Irish market over most of a decade.

**Fronius** selected Local Power as **listed by Fronius as a Fronius System Partner Plus (FSP+), and the only holder listed in Ireland on Fronius's partner directory as at September 2026**. Fronius states that FSP+ partners are hand-picked on demonstrated service standard and customer satisfaction. One company in Ireland holds it.

The pattern matters more than any single appointment. They appoint on evidence, and they have access to evidence a customer never sees — failure rates, claim patterns, commissioning quality across an installed base.

**When a customer chooses Local Power, they are inheriting the due diligence those manufacturers already performed.**

## 6. Warranties tested in practice, not read from a datasheet

Any installer can quote warranty terms from a brochure. Ten years of installing SOLARWATT and Fronius across 80 MWp+ means Local Power has administered real claims, on real failures, with both manufacturers — and knows how each behaves when something actually goes wrong.

That experience is why Local Power specifies what it specifies. The relevant mechanics:

- **Fronius FSP+ status** lets Local Power replace faulty parts and entire devices **on site**, without removing the inverter to process a warranty claim. Downtime is reduced accordingly — and this capability exists in Ireland only through Local Power.
- **SOLARWATT Premium Installer status** confers expedited warranty processing directly with the manufacturer.
- **SOLARWATT panel warranty terms** cover removal, installation and replacement costs — not just the component. A warranty that pays for the panel but not for the labour to fit it is a materially weaker instrument, and this distinction is invisible on a quotation.
- **Proactive monitoring** through Fronius Solar.web means faults are frequently detected and resolved before the customer is aware of them.

A warranty's value is not its headline duration. It is the combination of duration, what it actually covers, how the manufacturer behaves on a claim, and whether your installer can act on it quickly. Local Power's specification choices reflect ten years of observing all four.
