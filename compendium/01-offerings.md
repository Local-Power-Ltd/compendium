## 1. Solar PV

### Solar PV for Business
`https://localpower.ie/solar/solar-for-business/`

Commercial solar designed for return on investment rather than gesture. Roof-mounted, ground-mounted or hybrid, specified against actual consumption data.

| Approach | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Roof-mounted** | Warehouses, manufacturing facilities, commercial buildings. Lower installation cost, uses existing structures, faster delivery. |
| **Ground-mounted** | Sites with available land, businesses planning expansion. Independent of roof condition, optimised orientation for higher yield, easier maintenance access. |
| **Hybrid** | Large industrial sites, food production, distribution centres. Maximises generation across available space and avoids overloading a single asset. |

- **Typical payback:** 3–5 years, factoring in VAT refund, grants and prevailing electricity rates
- **Grants:** €2,700 to €162,600, typically covering 20–30% of project cost
- **Compliance:** full ESB Networks approval, EN 50549 compliant inverters, Safe Electric registered installation
- **Design life:** components rated for 20–30+ years

### Solar PV for Farms
`https://localpower.ie/solar/solar-for-farms/`

Irish farms have daytime electricity loads that align closely with solar generation — which is why farm solar paybacks tend to be short.

**Loads that match solar generation:** milking parlours, milk cooling (continuous daytime refrigeration), livestock and drying shed ventilation, cold storage, grain drying, water pumping and slurry management, workshop and office.

- **Typical payback:** 2–4.5 years, depending on grant route, entity type and consumption
- **Grant routes:** **SEAI at 20–25%, currently the practical route for most farms**, or **TAMS 3 at up to 60%**, which is difficult to secure at present. Local Power is registered with both the SEAI and the Department of Agriculture and advises on which is genuinely achievable — see `05-grants-and-incentives.md`
- **Tax:** accelerated capital allowances, allowing qualifying equipment to be written off in year one; the cash value depends on your tax position — applies on either grant route
- **TAMS ceilings where approved:** solar PV up to 62 kWp, battery storage up to 31 kWh, investment ceiling €90,000

**Add-ons frequently specified on farms:** hot water diverters (payback 1–2 years), battery storage for evening milking, EV and equipment charging readiness.

## 2. Battery Storage

### Commercial & Industrial Storage
`https://localpower.ie/storage/battery-storage-for-commercial-industrial/`

**Local Power has commercial and industrial battery storage projects operational in Ireland** — this is installed, commissioned, running capability rather than a proposition.

Four control strategies, selected against the customer's tariff structure and load profile:

| Strategy | Best for | Typical saving |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Solar self-consumption (rule-based)** | Businesses with solar PV and daytime consumption | 15–25% increase in solar self-consumption value |
| **2. Time-of-use arbitrage (optimised)** | ToU tariffs without significant demand charges | 20–30% reduction in energy costs |
| **3. Optimised ToU + demand control** | Both ToU tariffs and significant demand charges | 25–40% reduction in total electricity costs |
| **4. Peak shaving + energy arbitrage** | Large industrial sites, data centres, complex operations | 30–50% reduction in total electricity costs |

**Why demand charges matter.** Many Irish commercial tariffs bill on peak consumption measured over 15- or 30-minute intervals. Demand charges can be 30–50% of a total electricity bill. A battery discharging as consumption approaches the threshold can cut them by 30–60%.

*Worked example:* peak demand 200 kW at €25/kW/month = €5,000/month. Reduced to 140 kW = €3,500/month. Saving €1,500/month, €18,000/year.

**Typical Irish ToU structure:** night 11pm–8am €0.12–0.14/kWh · day 8am–5pm €0.22–0.26/kWh · peak 5pm–11pm €0.28–0.35/kWh.

- **Technology:** LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate), 10-year design life, integrated BMS, thermal management, fire detection and suppression
- **Typical payback:** 3–10 years depending on tariff, load profile and strategy
- **Delivery:** 8–16 weeks from approval
- **Partners:** Vilion (founded by the original BYD team), SOLARWATT
- **Sectors:** manufacturing, food production and cold storage, commercial offices, data centres

### Mobile Battery Storage — Hushh Pods
`https://localpower.ie/storage/mobile-battery-storage-hushh-pods/`

Hybrid generator replacement: a mobile battery unit that works alongside an existing diesel or HVO generator. The generator runs only to recharge the battery, at full load and full efficiency; the site runs silently from stored power in between.

- **Up to 50% reduction in generator runtime**
- Lower fuel consumption, reduced Scope 1 emissions, quantifiable data for ESG reporting
- Generator runs at optimal load rather than inefficient part-load, extending engine life and service intervals
- Silent operation between cycles — meets urban noise restrictions and planning conditions
- Industrial-grade lithium, weatherproof enclosure, integrated BMS, compatible with a wide range of diesel and HVO generators
- **Manufacturer:** Power Saving Solutions (UK, established 2013), with a 24/7 helpdesk supporting Local Power's field team
- **Available for hire or purchase**

*Worked example:* generator running 24 h/day at 5 l/h = 120 l/day. With a Hushh Pod, runtime falls to 10 h/day = 50 l/day. Saving 70 litres per day.

**Sectors:** construction, film and television production, events, cross-hire, urban development.

### Home Battery Storage

Options: SOLARWATT Battery vision (designed with BMW Designworks), My Energi Libbi, BYD.

Storage is what turns a solar array from a daytime generator into a system that covers the house around the clock. Without it, generation produced while nobody is home is exported at a fraction of what it costs to buy back that evening.

**What a battery adds:**

- Evening and overnight use of electricity generated during the day, instead of exporting it cheaply
- Resilience during outages
- The ability to charge from the grid at low overnight rates and discharge at peak — the mechanism that makes time-of-use and dynamic tariffs pay
- Higher self-consumption, which is where the value in an Irish solar system now sits

## 4. Biomethane
`https://localpower.ie/biomethane/`

Local Power is **Biogest's main partner in Ireland**, delivering complete anaerobic digestion and biomethane projects.

**The process:** feedstock (slurry, manures, food waste, grass silage, energy crops) → anaerobic digestion in sealed oxygen-free tanks → biogas at 60–70% methane → upgrading to >95% methane → grid injection or local use.

**Three outputs:** biomethane (renewable gas for heat, transport or electricity), digestate (nutrient-rich fertiliser superior to raw slurry), and heat.

**Why biomethane matters in Ireland:** it is dispatchable — it works 24/7 and is not weather-dependent — it uses existing gas grid infrastructure, it reduces agricultural methane emissions from slurry storage, and it replaces imported fossil gas with domestic renewable gas.

**Commercial models:**

| Participant | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| **Feedstock suppliers** | €15–35 per tonne for slurry, manures and energy crops; 10–20 year contracts; digestate returned; no capital investment required |
| **Land lease / hosting** | €5,000–15,000 per hectare per year; 20–30 year agreements; landowner retains ownership |
| **Industrial energy users** | Low-carbon renewable gas for heat and power; energy cost stability through long-term contracts; reduced Scope 1 and 2 emissions |
| **Data centres** | 24/7 renewable baseload; on-site CHP from biomethane; verifiable renewable gas for sustainability commitments |

**Local Power's role:** feasibility and site assessment, feedstock analysis, commercial modelling, planning permission, Gas Networks Ireland connection applications, grant and funding applications, engineering design, construction management, commissioning, and long-term operations and maintenance.

**Biogest** provides the anaerobic digestion reactor design, biogas upgrading systems, process control and global technical support, with a track record spanning Europe, North America and Australia.
