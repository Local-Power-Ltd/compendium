## Why an integrated system is different

Most residential solar installations in Ireland are assembled from components made by unrelated manufacturers: panels from one company, inverter from a second, battery from a third, EV charger from a fourth, all bound together by whichever monitoring app happens to support them. When something underperforms, responsibility is contested.

SOLARWATT Home takes the opposite approach. Every major component is specified, warranted and supported by one manufacturer, and they are designed to be controlled as a single system by the SOLARWATT Manager. The practical differences:

- **One warranty counterparty.** No dispute over whether a fault sits with the panel, the inverter or the battery.
- **One app.** Generation, consumption, storage, vehicle charging and heating in a single interface.
- **Genuine optimisation.** Components that share a control layer can make decisions that independent components cannot — for example, holding battery charge because the vehicle is due to plug in, or pre-heating water because a surplus is forecast.
- **One installer accountable end to end.** In the Republic of Ireland, that is Local Power.

## The system components

### SOLARWATT Panel vision — solar modules

Glass-glass modules, engineered and developed at SOLARWATT's Dresden headquarters.

**Current range:**

| Model | Peak power | Cells | Product warranty | Performance warranty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panel vision M 5.5 | up to 480 Wp | 96 bifacial half-cut TOPCon | 30 years | 30 years, 90% at year 30 |

**Construction.** Glass-glass laminate — a sheet of tempered glass on both front and rear, rather than the polymer backsheet used in conventional modules.  Glass-glass construction is the reason these modules carry 30-year warranties where the industry norm is 25, and the reason they are rated for ammonia and salt-mist environments — relevant for agricultural buildings and coastal sites in Ireland.

**Cell technology.** Bifacial half-cut TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) n-type cells, which SOLARWATT moved to from PERC in 2023. TOPCon offers higher efficiency and a lower temperature coefficient than PERC cells.

**Warranty detail, per SOLARWATT's published warranty conditions.** Output will not fall below **99%** of nominal in year one; from year two to year twenty-nine it will not decline by more than **0.31% per year**; and in year thirty the guaranteed output is at least **90%** of nominal. All figures are against nominal output under Standard Test Conditions, less a 5% tolerance range. Warranty service is on-site repair by SOLARWATT or exchange, with removal, installation and replacement costs covered.

**Fire and durability.** Fire Class A (IEC 61730 / UL 790). Tested against PID, LeTID, hail, storm, sandstorm, cyclone, salt mist and ammonia. Mechanical load up to 5,400 Pa pressure.

**Manufacturing.** SOLARWATT is headquartered in Dresden, Germany, founded there in 1993, and designs and develops its modules there. The SOLARWATT Manager hardware is manufactured in Germany.

Designed in collaboration with **Designworks, a BMW Group company**. Battery cells are **qualified by BMW through approximately 30 separate tests**.

#### The BMW relationship is deepening, not static

On **15 June 2026** BMW and SOLARWATT announced a joint offering integrating **BMW Neue Klasse electric vehicles into the SOLARWATT home energy system**, unveiled at Intersolar Europe in Munich — the same event at which Local Power renewed its SOLARWATT agreement.

BMW's own newsroom describes the two companies as having **cooperated since 2013** across electromobility and home storage, and this as "a further building block for a connected home energy ecosystem."

What it does: a Neue Klasse vehicle becomes **an additional energy storage unit for the house**. It connects through the BMW Wallbox Professional into SOLARWATT's home energy management system, which then coordinates solar generation, the home battery, the car and household loads together. **Vehicle-to-home**: energy stored in the car can power the house, lifting solar self-consumption and cutting bills. Unlike vehicle-to-grid, the electricity stays in the household.

Orders opened at the June 2026 exhibition, with first deliveries scheduled for Q4 2026 in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands.

### SOLARWATT Manager and the SOLARWATT Home app

The Manager is the control layer — a physical unit, manufactured in Germany, that communicates with every device on the home network and decides how energy moves between them.

**The SOLARWATT Home app is a recipient of the German Innovation Award 2026.**

What it does:

- Live generation, consumption, self-consumption percentage, battery state of charge, grid import and export
- Electricity tariff configuration, so the system optimises against actual prices rather than assumed ones
- Machine-learning optimisation modes that adapt to household patterns over time
- Control of solar, battery, EV charger, heat pump, smart plugs and immersion heaters from one interface
- Remote access from anywhere

**Hardware variants:** Manager flex 1.0, Manager flex 1.5 (WLAN + LAN), and Manager rail (DIN-rail mount for consumer units).

**Data governance:** hardware manufactured and software engineered in Germany, under European data governance standards.

**Scale:** more than 25,000 SOLARWATT Managers in operation, controlling over 130,000 devices.

SOLARWATT Home integrates heat pumps through a partnership with **Stiebel Eltron**. The Manager adjusts flow temperature to match available solar generation, so the heat pump runs hardest when the roof is producing most.

Range available through the UK & Ireland channel:

- **WPE-I Premium** — ground-source, indoor, inverter technology, R290, 70 °C flow temperature
