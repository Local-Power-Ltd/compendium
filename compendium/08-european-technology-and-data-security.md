# European Technology and Data Security

> **The short version.** Every system Local Power installs is controlled by European-manufactured hardware running European-engineered software: **Fronius** inverters, made in Austria, and the **SOLARWATT Manager**, manufactured and engineered in Germany. This is a deliberate specification choice, not an accident of sourcing, and it has consequences for data protection, grid security and long-term supportability that most solar buyers never think to ask about.

---

## Why the control layer is a security question

A modern solar installation is not passive equipment. The inverter and energy manager are **internet-connected control devices** with authority over how and when electricity flows in a building, and they receive remote firmware updates from the manufacturer for the life of the system — typically 10 to 30 years.

That means two things a buyer should understand:

1. **They generate and transmit data.** Consumption profiles, generation data, battery state, and — where EV charging or heat pumps are integrated — a detailed picture of occupancy and behaviour. For a business, half-hourly consumption data is a reasonably direct read on operational activity.
2. **They can be changed remotely, after installation.** Whoever controls the firmware channel controls the device, for as long as it is installed.

For a homeowner, this is a privacy consideration. For a business, a public body, or an operator of critical infrastructure, it is a supply chain and operational security consideration — and it is increasingly one that appears in procurement questionnaires.

## What Local Power specifies

### Fronius — Austrian inverters

Fronius manufactures in Austria. Local Power is **listed by Fronius as a Fronius System Partner Plus (FSP+), and the only holder listed in Ireland on Fronius's partner directory as at September 2026**, the highest partner tier, with direct access to Fronius technical support and the ability to replace devices on site without removing the inverter for a warranty claim.

Monitoring runs through **Fronius Solar.web**, an Austrian-operated platform. Local Power uses it for proactive monitoring — detecting and acting on faults before the customer is aware of them.

### SOLARWATT Manager — German hardware and software

SOLARWATT states the position directly:

> "Since we manufacture the hardware and engineer the software for each SOLARWATT Manager in Germany, we give you the peace of mind that your system is designed to meet the toughest European data governance standards."

The Manager is the control layer for the whole system — solar, battery, EV charger, heat pump, smart plugs, immersion heaters. It is the component with the most complete picture of what happens in the building, and it is German-made end to end.

SOLARWATT operates more than 25,000 Managers controlling over 130,000 devices.

## Why this matters commercially

**GDPR and data residency.** European-manufactured, European-operated systems are designed against European data protection law as a starting condition rather than adapted to it afterwards. For any organisation that has to answer questions about where its operational data is processed, this is a materially simpler answer to give.

**Public sector and regulated buyers.** Local Power's client list includes public bodies and infrastructure operators — Teagasc, Translink. Buyers in this class increasingly ask about supply chain provenance and remote access to connected equipment. Being able to answer "Austrian inverter, German energy manager, European platform" is a clean answer.

**Long-term supportability.** A 30-year warranty needs a manufacturer that is still there and still shipping firmware in year 25. Fronius and SOLARWATT are long-established European manufacturers — SOLARWATT founded 1993, majority-owned by Stefan Quandt since 1996. Warranty service and parts routing stay inside the EU.

**Warranty enforceability.** A European manufacturer with European legal presence is a counterparty a customer can realistically pursue. This is the practical substance behind the phrase "European warranties" that appears across Local Power's materials.

## What Local Power does not do

Local Power specifies European inverters and energy management as standard, rather than selecting the lowest-cost control hardware available. This is a higher-cost specification. The reasoning given is consistent with the company's broader position:

> "Innovation with Proven Technology — Leading edge, not bleeding edge"

> "We design for decades, not just today"

The control layer is where a system's long-term behaviour, security posture and supportability are determined, and it is the component a customer is least equipped to evaluate for themselves.
