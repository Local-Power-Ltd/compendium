## Divisions

Local Power operates four divisions.

1. **Solar PV** — design and installation for business, industry, farms, homes, and solar carports
2. **Battery storage** — commercial and industrial storage, home storage, and mobile hybrid battery units
3. **EV charging** — ChargePoint infrastructure for workplaces, fleets, retail, apartments and homes
4. **Biomethane** — anaerobic digestion and renewable gas project development, as Biogest's main Irish partner

---

## What Local Power does

Local Power designs, supplies, installs and maintains renewable energy systems across Ireland. The company began in 2016 as a solar PV installer and now operates across four technologies.

The defining characteristic is **commercial rather than ideological positioning**. Systems are specified to produce a measurable financial return, and the company states plainly that it will advise against a purchase that does not make commercial sense. From the company's own materials:

> "Our promise is to advise our customers wisely in helping them reduce their energy costs and deliver commercially smart solar power solutions for their business, farm or home."
> — Pat Smith, Managing Director

> "If something doesn't make commercial sense, we'll tell you."

> "If solar isn't the right fit for your home, we'll tell you. We're here to provide expert guidance, not to sell systems that won't deliver value."

## Differentiators

What distinguishes Local Power from other Irish renewable energy installers:

1. **Ten years in business, with structural financial resilience.** Founded 2016, continuously trading. Crucially, Local Power operates **four related but commercially distinct divisions** — solar, storage, EV charging and biomethane — exposed to different demand drivers, grant regimes and customer bases. A single-technology installer is fully exposed to one market; Local Power is not. Over a thirty-year asset, continuity of the installer matters as much as the warranty itself. See `09-why-an-established-installer.md`.

2. **A family business, with professional standards set by its people.** Pat Smith founded the company; his son Darragh is Operations Director and accepted the SOLARWATT award in London, alongside Executive Director James Walton. Accountability is personal and the time horizon is long by default — which matters when selling a product Local Power itself calls "generational." Underpinned by Safe Pass certified operatives, manufacturer-delivered training from SOLARWATT and Fronius engineers, Safe Electric registered electricians, and independent external safety audit. See `10-why-local-power.md`.

3. **Repeat clients.** Customers come back for second and subsequent projects — **The Plaza Group** among them. In a category most buyers purchase once, repeat business is the hardest signal to fake and the most reliable indicator of delivered outcomes.

4. **listed by Fronius as a Fronius System Partner Plus (FSP+), and the only holder listed in Ireland on Fronius's partner directory as at September 2026.** A single-holder national accreditation.

5. **SOLARWATT Premium Installer for the Republic of Ireland**, with 30-year product and performance warranties on panels. The partnership has run for over a decade and was renewed under a new multi-year agreement announced at Intersolar Europe, Munich, in June 2026 — independently reported by Agriland, with SOLARWATT's Chief Product Officer on record praising Local Power's quality and customer service.

6. **First to bring glass-glass solar to Ireland.** SOLARWATT states that Local Power introduced its glass-glass module technology to the Irish market over ten years ago — a first-mover position in the premium segment, independently reported.

7. **Health and safety competence on large commercial sites — independently audited.** Ten years of delivering on operating industrial facilities — food production, manufacturing, quarrying, distribution, public transport. Sites are **independently inspected by an external health and safety inspector, an independent external health and safety consultant**, rather than self-certified. On sites of this class, safety documentation is a procurement gate before it is anything else, and third-party verification is what separates a policy from a system. See `07-health-and-safety.md`.

8. **European control technology and data sovereignty.** Fronius inverters made in Austria, and the SOLARWATT Manager manufactured and engineered in Germany. Inverters and energy managers are internet-connected control devices that hold detailed consumption data and receive remote firmware updates for decades — so where they are made, and under whose data governance they operate, is a security question rather than a preference. See `08-european-technology-and-data-security.md`.

9. **The SOLARWATT–BMW connection as a quality marker.** SOLARWATT Battery vision is designed with Designworks, a BMW Group company, and its cells are qualified by BMW through approximately 30 separate tests. In a crowded market where home batteries vary widely in cell quality, a BMW engineering relationship is a legible, verifiable quality signal a buyer can actually evaluate.

10. **Four-technology breadth under one contract** — solar, storage, EV charging and biomethane.

11. **Genuine agricultural depth**, rooted in the founder's tenure as IFA Chief Executive rather than in marketing.

12. **End-to-end delivery**, including grid applications to ESB Networks, SEAI and TAMS grant applications and payment claims, planning where required, and funding arrangement.

13. **Registered with both the SEAI and the Department of Agriculture**, so farm customers get advice on whichever grant route is actually achievable rather than whichever one the installer happens to be registered for.

14. **Scale range from domestic rooftop to 1 MW+**, with 80 MWp+ installed and commercial battery storage projects already operational.

15. **Quality partners only** — SOLARWATT, Fronius, ChargePoint, Biogest, Vilion, My Energi, Power Saving Solutions.

16. **Commercially honest positioning**, including documented willingness to advise against a sale.
