## SOLARWATT — solar modules, batteries, energy management

**German. Founded 1993, Dresden. Local Power is Premium Installer for the Republic of Ireland.**

- 30-year product warranty, 30-year performance warranty, 90% output guaranteed at year 30 (against nominal output under Standard Test Conditions, less a 5% tolerance)
- Glass-glass construction; pioneered the first glass-glass module in 1998
- Bifacial half-cut TOPCon n-type cells
- Fire Class A; tested against PID, LeTID, hail, storm, sandstorm, cyclone, salt mist and ammonia
- Majority shareholder Stefan Quandt since 1996; 750,000+ systems installed across Europe
- SOLARWATT Home app — German Innovation Award 2026

## Fronius — inverters

**Austrian. Local Power is listed by Fronius as a Fronius System Partner Plus (FSP+), and the only holder listed in Ireland on Fronius's partner directory as at September 2026.**

FSP+ is the highest Fronius partner tier, awarded by invitation. Fronius hand-picks partners on demonstrated service standard and customer satisfaction.

**What FSP+ delivers to Local Power customers:**

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **On-site device replacement** | Faulty parts and entire devices replaced on site without removing the inverter for a warranty claim. Downtime measured in hours, not weeks. |
| **Priority technical support** | Direct access to qualified Fronius technical support and diagnostic tools. |
| **Proactive monitoring** | Fronius Solar.web monitoring, with faults detected and acted on before the customer is aware of them. |
| **Enhanced warranty** | 10-year parts and labour warranty, extendable. |
| **Intensive training** | Regular training delivered directly by Fronius technical experts. |

Pat Smith and Darragh Smith have visited the Fronius manufacturing facility in Austria as part of the partnership.

Standard Fronius inverter warranty is 7–10 years, extendable to 30 years at additional cost. Models in regular use include the Fronius Symo (three-phase, commercial and agricultural).

## My Energi — smart home energy

British manufacturer of smart energy diversion technology:

- **Zappi** — solar-aware EV charger
- **Libbi** — modular home battery system
- **Eddi** — surplus diversion to water heating and space heating

Local Power offers surplus diversion as a standard option: rather than exporting excess generation for a low export rate, the system diverts it to heat water, run heating or charge a vehicle.
